Our story

Strong and Agile Management is the key to success in any business. Our widespread goodwill and exclusive quality of products can be attributed to our able management. Our Directors possesses more than 10 years of experience in the field of metals and metal perforation. Our real strength lies in our strong management foundation and effective management practices. Our highly experienced management and team members put stress on very high standards of clinical quality control of the perforated metals.

We have full range of manufacturing fabrication and finishing capabilities, which is a unique combination in the field of metal perforating. We perforate a wide range of metals with many different patterns. We also perforate metals according to your customized requirements

What we do

We manufacture wear parts for crushing, screening and conveying equipment. Our range covers concaves, mantles, jaw plates, cheek plates, wedges, rollers, idler rollers, wire mesh and shaft wear rings.

Because we have NDT, machining and testing under one roof, we can check quality at every stage instead of relying on outside labs.
