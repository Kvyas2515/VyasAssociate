What we do

We manufacture wear parts for crushing, screening and conveying equipment. Our range covers concaves, mantles, jaw plates, cheek plates, wedges, rollers, idler rollers, wire mesh and shaft wear rings.

Because we have NDT, machining and testing under one roof, we can check quality at every stage instead of relying on outside labs.
